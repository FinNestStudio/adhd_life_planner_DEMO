## What it is

A planner app for your phone and your computer that shows what matters right
now: your next small step, today's three, and your day on a clock. "Just 5
minutes" and "Stuck?" help you start, Gentle day and "Running late?" help when
the day goes sideways, and a fresh start welcomes you back after a few days
away, with no streaks to break and nothing that says "overdue". There's a brain
dump, habits, routines played one step at a time, goals, notes and a weekly reset.

The demo is the real app, opened on an example week so there is something to
explore straight away. You can add up to 30 things of your own before it asks
you to buy, and save a backup that the full app opens with everything in place.

## Your data stays yours

Everything you type is held in your own browser and goes nowhere else: no
server, no account, no tracking.

The full app adds your phone and computer kept in step (locked so only your
devices can read it) and installs on your Home Screen to work with no signal.
No subscription, pay once.
